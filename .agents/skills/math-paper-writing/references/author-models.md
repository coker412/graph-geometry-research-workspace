# Optional models from Tao papers

These editorial options come from a 2026-11-02 study of twenty Tao papers: eighteen research papers/notes and two surveys, first submitted during 2005–2022, with later revisions where recorded. The study used full texts, individual structure annotations, selected close reading, extraction statistics and five visually inspected papers. It did not audit all proofs or independently validate stylistic fidelity. These options do not define a mandatory author imitation.

## Tao corpus: explain reformulations and obligations

| Choice | Direct evidence | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain why an imported method addresses this obstacle | [Heisenberg embedding](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09223), pp. 3–5 | The derivative-loss analogy motivates Nash–Moser-type ideas; it does not license claiming a standard theorem applies verbatim |
| Give a dictionary and a route back to the original result | [Ionescu–Wainger and adeles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.05066), pp. 1–2, 9; [Regularity lemma revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0504472), pp. 9–10 | New terminology must do work; preserve the target manuscript's notation |
| Label a heuristic's simplifications and identify its formal replacement | [Collatz](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03562), heuristic/proposition in the introduction; [Kneser inverse theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04337), p. 4 | An informal model cannot discharge an unproved implication |
| Return through the actual reduction chain at closure | Collatz, final proof paragraph; Heisenberg embedding, final proof paragraph | Useful for a long argument; not a demand for repetitive recaps |
| State what a modified-system counterexample does and does not settle | [Averaged Navier–Stokes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.0290), p. 5; [Defocusing Schrödinger system](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00526), p. 5 | Related papers reduce independent stylistic evidence; preserve scalar/system and forcing distinctions |

Counterweights: [near-harmonic square packing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03594) is a short construction with a compact explanation of the perimeter bottleneck. The [Poincaré survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610903) declares a particular audience and omissions; its shortcuts are not a research-proof template. Longer sentences or recurring asides are not targets.

## Use and confidence

The choices above are repeated observations or explicit local examples in a selected corpus, not statistical author identification. No unseen-paper or independent fidelity test has been performed. The useful synthesis is: state the obstacle, provide the required interface, distinguish explanation from proof, and return to the original target. These sharpen existing exposition guidance without prescribing a fixed introduction, proof length, example count or typography.

Original papers remain available through their links. Recheck the source version when a cited page has moved. No full paper text, private writing sample, external service or author-profile file outside this skill is required to use the [distillation workflow](style-distillation.md).
