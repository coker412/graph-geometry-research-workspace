The corpus includes PDE, geometry, harmonic analysis, combinatorics, number theory and operator theory. It is a deliberately selected cross-field sample, not exclusively PDE or recent mature-period writing. Full texts, structural annotations and selected close reading support these reports; no exhaustive proof audit or independent author-fidelity test is claimed. All twenty informed the profile; there is no unseen comparison paper.

For targeted borrowing, read the relevant decision and source passages; the [short examples](author-models.md) provide a quick start. Full-profile drafting reads all five reports and five suitable full sources, obtained through the recorded version links. Keep the target paper’s hypotheses, notation, accepted abstract and proof status. See the [distillation workflow](style-distillation.md) for using or extending a profile.
