Method: OCR/embedded text; remove publisher covers, page tags, recurring page headers and trailing references; select blank-line blocks with >=25 English word tokens and >=65% alphabetic characters among nonspace characters. Markers are exact untagged lower-case word forms, not parts of speech. Sentence proxy splits on .!? followed by whitespace; abbreviations/formulas remain confounders. Each paper has equal weight in summaries; no author ranking or editorial length target.

Limits: Extraction is heterogeneous and OCR can corrupt formulas, punctuation and prose. These are partial L1 diagnostics; no validated syntactic/POS analysis. Formula-heavy blocks are preferentially omitted. Source-boundary cropping excludes adjacent unrelated articles. Twenty works is a workflow count, not twenty independent statistical observations. OCR punctuation is too unstable for an author-level punctuation rule; syntax is assessed through the cited transitions above. Word forms are not POS-tagged nouns or verbs. These partial statistics do not claim a complete linguistic analysis. G14, where applicable, is restricted to Milnor's signed letter; collaborative papers otherwise retain unknown passage attribution.

For application, change a relation between sentences—purpose, operation, consequence—before changing vocabulary. Preserve technical terms and the researcher's established notation.
