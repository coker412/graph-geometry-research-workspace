# Milnor: Writing DNA synthesis

[Profile entry](../../profile-milnor.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Classical differential geometry and geometric topology, 1953–1978. Twenty distinct complete works; 15 sole-authored and 5 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

The selection includes early work, surgery and exotic-sphere families, joint papers with Kervaire, Bott, Spanier and Fox, one published correspondence, a research proof note and a survey. It is not a mature-period-only sample or a geometric-flow template. Bott’s labeled reply in G14 is excluded from Milnor-only language diagnostics.

**Best use:** Expose the precise bridge between a geometric construction, an invariant and its consequence.

## Working choices

1. **Interpret the quantity that carries the proof.** G15 begins with an abstract closure question. Do not manufacture a geometric example before every definition.
2. **Separate obstruction, recognition and realization.** A calculated invariant does not by itself establish existence or the topological recognition step.
3. **Spell out the bridge to the auxiliary problem and back.** A common endpoint or a named correspondence is not a proof that the required maps agree.
4. **Keep the nearest meaningful distinction.** The distinction may be essential scope, not defensive writing. Dense G-delta, full measure, bundle isomorphism and spin-structure equivalence cannot be interchanged.

The [cognitive framework](cognitive-framework.md) gives the supporting page locations and strength of each observation. Repetition in a selected corpus is not statistical validation.

## Use in a new manuscript

For a local edit, choose one or two decisions that solve the actual exposition problem and read their cited passages. For full profile-guided drafting, read all five reports and five complete source works closest to the target's topic and genre, following [the mathematical adaptation](../../style-distillation.md). Download availability alone does not count as reading. Preserve the target's mathematics, notation, approved abstract and venue conventions.

Write a short trial with a fixed content contract. Compare assumptions, quantifiers, objects, parameter ranges, citations and proof status first; then compare clarity and economy. Do not paste the profile's confidence or metadata into the manuscript. If the source text is unavailable, describe the result as profile-only guidance.

## What changed and what remains optional

The expanded sample strengthens the separation of obstruction and construction. G12 and G18 supply direct checks against overaggressive removal of necessary warnings and scope. G14 also forces explicit handling of another author’s contribution.

These choices remain an optional author/corpus profile. The shared writing skill already covers precise scope, useful examples and proof interfaces. The expansion does not add a mandatory house style or extra rewrite pass. The [pilot applications](../../profile-applications.md) remain constructed same-agent examples; they do not establish an independent advantage of this expanded profile or isolate anti-defensive-writing.
