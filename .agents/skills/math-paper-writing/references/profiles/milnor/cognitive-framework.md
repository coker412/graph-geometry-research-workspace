# Milnor: question, evidence and reasoning presentation (L3–L5)

[Profile entry](../../profile-milnor.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Classical differential geometry and geometric topology, 1953–1978. Twenty distinct complete works; 15 sole-authored and 5 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

The selection includes early work, surgery and exotic-sphere families, joint papers with Kervaire, Bott, Spanier and Fox, one published correspondence, a research proof note and a survey. It is not a mature-period-only sample or a geometric-flow template. Bott’s labeled reply in G14 is excluded from Milnor-only language diagnostics.

## Question framing (L3)

Expose the precise bridge between a geometric construction, an invariant and its consequence. The [individual architectures](structure-patterns.md) identify whether the gap is an unavailable estimate, an ambiguous formulation, a new invariant, a stronger identity, an obstruction or an alternative proof. Infer the article's stated problem from the text; do not infer an author's universal research agenda.

## Evidence strategy (L4)

For each borrowed passage, identify the job of the evidence: cited theorem supplies an input; construction proves existence; calculation evaluates a defined invariant; counterexample separates claims; numerical evidence suggests a question. The corpus annotations name important distinctions. A prestigious source or visually persuasive example does not replace the required implication. Collaborations and related series must be reported as such.

## Reasoning presentation (L5)

For a packing argument, identify the separated points, disjoint neighborhoods, containing region and both measure bounds. For an invariant obstruction, distinguish impossibility from construction of a realizing example.

Choose an explanatory order that follows the target proof's dependencies. Keep formal arguments, heuristic analogies and unresolved steps distinguishable at their point of use. The rules describe observable exposition, not psychological traits.

## Change from the initial small sample

The expanded sample strengthens the separation of obstruction and construction. G12 and G18 supply direct checks against overaggressive removal of necessary warnings and scope. G14 also forces explicit handling of another author’s contribution.
