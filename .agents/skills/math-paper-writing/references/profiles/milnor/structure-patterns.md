# Milnor: structure patterns (L2)

[Profile entry](../../profile-milnor.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Classical differential geometry and geometric topology, 1953–1978. Twenty distinct complete works; 15 sole-authored and 5 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

The selection includes early work, surgery and exotic-sphere families, joint papers with Kervaire, Bott, Spanier and Fox, one published correspondence, a research proof note and a survey. It is not a mature-period-only sample or a geometric-flow template. Bott’s labeled reply in G14 is excluded from Milnor-only language diagnostics.

## Recurring architectures

| Architecture | Works | Appropriate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantity → comparison → geometric consequence | G1, G2, G10, G17 | Useful when the geometric meaning would otherwise vanish inside an estimate or invariant. |
| Obstruction and realization → distinct objects | G3, G5, G6, G9, G11, G19, G20 | Prove both interfaces before claiming sharpness or classification. |
| Operational definition → construction or classification | G8, G12, G15, G16, G18 | Sometimes the definition itself identifies the obstacle. Abstract openings are allowed. |
| Exposition adapted to genre | G7, G13, G14, G21 | Conference argument, alternative-proof note, correspondence and long survey have different pacing. |

Use these as dependency patterns. Do not impose a fixed number of sections, examples or open questions. Related sequels remain correlated evidence.
