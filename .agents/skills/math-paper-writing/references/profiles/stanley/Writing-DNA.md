# Stanley: Writing DNA synthesis

[Profile entry](../../profile-stanley.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Enumerative and algebraic combinatorics, 1996–2016. Twenty distinct complete works; 12 sole-authored and 8 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

Several works share symmetric-function and tableau machinery. Coauthored articles include major contributions by others; the profile does not allocate sentences. Manuscript revision dates can differ from publication years and the author index can point to a changed version. This is not a broad sample of extremal or probabilistic combinatorics.

**Best use:** Keep counting objects, encodings and recovered statistics connected.

## Working choices

1. **Define the encoding by its mathematical job.** S16 opens with Schubert degrees and S18 states specialist prerequisites. A toy example is not compulsory.
2. **When a bijection is the proof, expose its inverse.** S3 and S10 use other proofs and ask for bijective explanations. Do not relabel an algebraic proof as a bijection.
3. **Read a useful consequence back out of the formula.** Include the specialization that serves this paper, not a quota of examples. Keep asymptotic regimes separate in S2.
4. **Locate the unresolved part precisely.** S7 closes its asymptotic proof and S9 gives a reformulation. Neither needs an invented open question.

The [cognitive framework](cognitive-framework.md) gives the supporting page locations and strength of each observation. Repetition in a selected corpus is not statistical validation.

## Use in a new manuscript

For a local edit, choose one or two decisions that solve the actual exposition problem and read their cited passages. For full profile-guided drafting, read all five reports and five complete source works closest to the target's topic and genre, following [the mathematical adaptation](../../style-distillation.md). Download availability alone does not count as reading. Preserve the target's mathematics, notation, approved abstract and venue conventions.

Write a short trial with a fixed content contract. Compare assumptions, quantifiers, objects, parameter ranges, citations and proof status first; then compare clarity and economy. Do not paste the profile's confidence or metadata into the manuscript. If the source text is unavailable, describe the result as profile-only guidance.

## What changed and what remains optional

The expanded sample rejects always begin with a concrete toy example. It strengthens the inverse-map lesson and the need to distinguish a proved identity from a desired alternative proof.

These choices remain an optional author/corpus profile. The shared writing skill already covers precise scope, useful examples and proof interfaces. The expansion does not add a mandatory house style or extra rewrite pass. The [pilot applications](../../profile-applications.md) remain constructed same-agent examples; they do not establish an independent advantage of this expanded profile or isolate anti-defensive-writing.
