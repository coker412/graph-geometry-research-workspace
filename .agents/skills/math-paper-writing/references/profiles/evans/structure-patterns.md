# Evans: structure patterns (L2)

[Profile entry](../../profile-evans.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Nonlinear PDE and variational methods, 1985–2017. Twenty distinct complete works; 5 sole-authored and 15 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

The infinity-Laplacian papers, the two effective-Hamiltonian papers, and the two level-set papers form related families. E3 contains formal continuum reasoning; E19 is an expository research announcement. Collaborations do not establish sentence-level authorship.

## Recurring architectures

| Architecture | Works | Appropriate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tool → estimate → payoff | E1, E2, E10, E12, E18 | Best for a new analytic device whose benefit otherwise appears late. |
| Regularize → control → limit → interpret | E7, E9, E11, E17, E20, E21 | Best when existence or asymptotics hinges on a particular compactness or error estimate. |
| Change the formulation → recover the motion/dynamics | E5, E6, E13, E14, E15, E16 | Best when the old solution concept or smooth transformation fails. Several entries are related sequels, not independent replications. |
| Compare representations or models | E3, E8, E19 | Best for formal connections or expository research; carry their distinct proof status. |

Use these as dependency patterns. Do not impose a fixed number of sections, examples or open questions. Related sequels remain correlated evidence.
