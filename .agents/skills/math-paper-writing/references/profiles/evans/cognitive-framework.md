# Evans: question, evidence and reasoning presentation (L3–L5)

[Profile entry](../../profile-evans.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Nonlinear PDE and variational methods, 1985–2017. Twenty distinct complete works; 5 sole-authored and 15 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

The infinity-Laplacian papers, the two effective-Hamiltonian papers, and the two level-set papers form related families. E3 contains formal continuum reasoning; E19 is an expository research announcement. Collaborations do not establish sentence-level authorship.

## Question framing (L3)

Make the job of an auxiliary equation, estimate or limiting argument visible. The [individual architectures](structure-patterns.md) identify whether the gap is an unavailable estimate, an ambiguous formulation, a new invariant, a stronger identity, an obstruction or an alternative proof. Infer the article's stated problem from the text; do not infer an author's universal research agenda.

## Evidence strategy (L4)

For each borrowed passage, identify the job of the evidence: cited theorem supplies an input; construction proves existence; calculation evaluates a defined invariant; counterexample separates claims; numerical evidence suggests a question. The corpus annotations name important distinctions. A prestigious source or visually persuasive example does not replace the required implication. Collaborations and related series must be reported as such.

## Reasoning presentation (L5)

If a compactness argument lists several convergences, identify which term each convergence controls. Preserve subsequence/full-sequence distinctions and every assumed bound.

Choose an explanatory order that follows the target proof's dependencies. Keep formal arguments, heuristic analogies and unresolved steps distinguishable at their point of use. The rules describe observable exposition, not psychological traits.

## Change from the initial small sample

The three-paper pilot overrepresented adjoint methods. E5 supplies an explicit alternative; E9 and E17 broaden support for tracking the role of estimates. No adjoint equation, mandatory first corollary or limitations paragraph is prescribed.
