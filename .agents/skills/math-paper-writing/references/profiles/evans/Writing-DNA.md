# Evans: Writing DNA synthesis

[Profile entry](../../profile-evans.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Nonlinear PDE and variational methods, 1985–2017. Twenty distinct complete works; 5 sole-authored and 15 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

The infinity-Laplacian papers, the two effective-Hamiltonian papers, and the two level-set papers form related families. E3 contains formal continuum reasoning; E19 is an expository research announcement. Collaborations do not establish sentence-level authorship.

**Best use:** Make the job of an auxiliary equation, estimate or limiting argument visible.

## Working choices

1. **State what the tool makes possible.** E5 pp. 2–3 replaces an earlier adjoint proof with a maximum-principle route. Borrow the tool-to-job connection, not a preferred tool.
2. **Show an estimate earning its place.** A long construction such as E9 cannot supply every payoff immediately. State the eventual job rather than fabricate a short corollary.
3. **Name the exact missing property before changing approach.** Do not turn historical obstacles into claims about the current literature. E15 presents a new proof of a known result without inventing a failure of that theorem.
4. **Return from estimates to the original object.** Retain all convergence modes, data assumptions and limit orders. A formal return in E3 or E19 is not a rigorous existence claim.

The [cognitive framework](cognitive-framework.md) gives the supporting page locations and strength of each observation. Repetition in a selected corpus is not statistical validation.

## Use in a new manuscript

For a local edit, choose one or two decisions that solve the actual exposition problem and read their cited passages. For full profile-guided drafting, read all five reports and five complete source works closest to the target's topic and genre, following [the mathematical adaptation](../../style-distillation.md). Download availability alone does not count as reading. Preserve the target's mathematics, notation, approved abstract and venue conventions.

Write a short trial with a fixed content contract. Compare assumptions, quantifiers, objects, parameter ranges, citations and proof status first; then compare clarity and economy. Do not paste the profile's confidence or metadata into the manuscript. If the source text is unavailable, describe the result as profile-only guidance.

## What changed and what remains optional

The three-paper pilot overrepresented adjoint methods. E5 supplies an explicit alternative; E9 and E17 broaden support for tracking the role of estimates. No adjoint equation, mandatory first corollary or limitations paragraph is prescribed.

These choices remain an optional author/corpus profile. The shared writing skill already covers precise scope, useful examples and proof interfaces. The expansion does not add a mandatory house style or extra rewrite pass. The [pilot applications](../../profile-applications.md) remain constructed same-agent examples; they do not establish an independent advantage of this expanded profile or isolate anti-defensive-writing.
