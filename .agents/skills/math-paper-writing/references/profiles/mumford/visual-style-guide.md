## Transferable choices

Use a visual when it exposes a correspondence, construction, changing parameter or distinction that is costly to reconstruct from prose. Give each visual that specific job. Label maps and their domains, diagram conventions, parameters, and the mathematical meaning of table entries. A commutative diagram still needs a reason for commutativity; a numerical plot remains numerical evidence.

Some sampled works have no pictorial figures. Zero extracted raster images is not evidence of no figures: vector drawings, displayed arrays and uncaptioned diagrams also count. Historical font choices, scanning artifacts, publisher headers and journal page designs are not author-level recommendations. Follow the target manuscript's format and accessibility needs.

When a new subject requires a different visual, choose it for the mathematics. Do not reproduce source figures, copy their stylistic signature or add a diagram merely to resemble the author.
