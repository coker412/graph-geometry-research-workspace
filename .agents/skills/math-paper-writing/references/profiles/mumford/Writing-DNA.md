# Mumford: Writing DNA synthesis

[Profile entry](../../profile-mumford.md) · [Synthesis](Writing-DNA.md) · [Corpus and structures](structure-patterns.md)

**Scope:** Classical algebraic geometry, 1961–1982. Twenty distinct complete works; 12 sole-authored and 8 collaborative. The four-author study keeps one additional prior pilot comparison per author outside these twenty. Complete files were available; selected openings, transitions, endings and structural features were read. This is not an exhaustive proof audit or an independently validated author model.

The sample includes related degeneration constructions and collaborations with Matsusaka, Newstead, Deligne, Harris, Oort, Horrocks, Artin and Bombieri. It is not all modern algebraic geometry or Mumford’s later work in other fields. A paper by Ness with a Mumford appendix was excluded instead of counting it as his coauthored research paper.

**Best use:** Explain why a formulation or change of category makes the original question tractable.

## Working choices

1. **Resolve the meaningful ambiguity before proving a claim.** Do not reintroduce standard definitions when the target manuscript already fixes them.
2. **Make a larger framework earn its complexity.** M12 explicitly refers to companion motivation. Do not force a self-contained motivational preamble into every sequel.
3. **Use explanatory order rather than discovery order.** This is an optional organizational choice, not a claim about how Mumford usually thinks or how the coauthors divided their work.
4. **Close the dictionary and distinguish the original properties.** A nearby counterexample must already be justified. Do not infer extra fibers, characteristic ranges or classification claims from a fluent explanation.

The [cognitive framework](cognitive-framework.md) gives the supporting page locations and strength of each observation. Repetition in a selected corpus is not statistical validation.

## Use in a new manuscript

For a local edit, choose one or two decisions that solve the actual exposition problem and read their cited passages. For full profile-guided drafting, read all five reports and five complete source works closest to the target's topic and genre, following [the mathematical adaptation](../../style-distillation.md). Download availability alone does not count as reading. Preserve the target's mathematics, notation, approved abstract and venue conventions.

Write a short trial with a fixed content contract. Compare assumptions, quantifiers, objects, parameter ranges, citations and proof status first; then compare clarity and economy. Do not paste the profile's confidence or metadata into the manuscript. If the source text is unavailable, describe the result as profile-only guidance.

## What changed and what remains optional

The larger sample adds an explicit discovery-versus-presentation example and a contrary case for repeatedly re-explaining motivation. Core instructions on useful definitions and returning from reductions already cover much of the common craft.

These choices remain an optional author/corpus profile. The shared writing skill already covers precise scope, useful examples and proof interfaces. The expansion does not add a mandatory house style or extra rewrite pass. The [pilot applications](../../profile-applications.md) remain constructed same-agent examples; they do not establish an independent advantage of this expanded profile or isolate anti-defensive-writing.
