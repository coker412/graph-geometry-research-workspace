# Tao-corpus writing profile

**Scope:** twenty selected sole-authored papers, including eighteen research papers/notes and two surveys. The corpus spans several mathematical fields and includes older expository anchors; it is not a portrait of only recent Tao writing. Full-text acquisition, structural annotation, selected close reading and a five-paper visual sample are complete. This is not mathematical certification or independently validated author identification.

## Decisions to borrow

| Decision | Evidence | Apply when / exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name the exact bottleneck that motivates an imported method | [T05](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09223), pp. 3–5; [T07](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03594), p. 2 | The mechanism explains the improvement; do not add another field merely for sophistication |
| Give the correspondence into another language and the route back | [T17](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.05066), pp. 1–2, 9; [T19](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0504472), pp. 9–10 | A reformulation does real work; retain the original manuscript's conventions and define only necessary new objects |
| Mark the simplifications in an informal overview and locate their formal replacement | [T01](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03562), pp. 5–6; [T13](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.01908), pp. 3–4; [T16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04337), p. 4 | A heuristic genuinely helps; it cannot discharge a proof obligation |
| Close a chain of reductions by returning to the original target | T01, p. 56; T05, p. 45; [T11](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08059), p. 31 | The argument has several interfaces; short proofs need no ceremonial recap |
| Keep a barrier result separate from the original open problem | [T09](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.0290), p. 5; [T12](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00526), p. 5 | The result concerns an altered equation or larger class; preserve all scope distinctions |

These are repeated corpus observations with known family/genre dependencies. They have not been tested on unseen papers or by an independent reader.

## Do not turn these into rules

Do not imitate long abstracts, repeated verbal signatures, jokes, parenthetical density, or arbitrary notation. Do not import survey omissions into a research proof. T07 is a compact construction; T18/T20 are audience-specific surveys. A user's accepted short abstract and locked notation remain binding.

Use with [language observations](language-dna.md), [structure patterns](structure-patterns.md), [framing and evidence](cognitive-framework.md), and [visual evidence](visual-style-guide.md). A profile supplies choices appropriate to the new paper, not an instruction to simulate Tao's identity.
