# Visual evidence: the selected Tao corpus

Five papers were sampled: T02, T04, T07, T14 and T19. Full-document raster/vector/caption scans located candidate figures; first pages and all identified figure pages were rendered and opened. The table records the inspected pages; obtain the corresponding version through the [source index](structure-patterns.md#source-index). Source PDFs and review screenshots are not redistributed.

| Paper | PDF pages opened | Figure's role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T02 | 1, 5 | Colored spacetime schematic locates concentrations, regularity regions and propagation steps; the long caption narrates the proof strategy |
| T04 | 1, 8 | Vector-field plot illustrates a dynamical obstruction; caption specifies identification of opposite sides |
| T07 | 1, 10 | Packing diagram identifies the square arrangement and leftover strips used by the construction |
| T14 | 1 | No figures identified in the document scan; journal-template front matter differs from other samples |
| T19 | 1 | No figures identified in the document scan; prose and displays carry the reformulation |

All three identified substantive figures were inspected. Each has a mathematical role. None establishes that a proof is valid from visual intuition alone. For an adaptation of T02, retain labels and shapes that identify the regions even without color; its particular green/purple palette is not a style requirement. T07's labels connect a picture to specific quantities rather than substituting the picture for their definitions.

Typography varies between preprint and journal templates. T14 even has placeholder journal metadata in the sampled PDF, which must not be copied. This is evidence against treating fonts, dates or page furniture as the author's personal visual signature. The sample covers five papers only; visual claims about the remaining fifteen are unverified.
