# Delivery checks

Apply the checks relevant to the edited material and its affected dependencies. A local wording edit does not require unrelated submission or figure checks. Repository requirements for declaring a whole paper finished still apply.

First compare the delivered draft or revision against the current request and inherited author decisions: mathematical source, requested scope, approved template, protected content, declarations and output locations. Use direct artifact evidence, and diff evidence when revising an existing manuscript; identify what was not checked. Reading this skill, matching a class name, passing a build, or passing a file-location check cannot substitute for that comparison. Reuse existing check records rather than generating another report tree.

Check, in proportion to the task:

1. formal statements against their source of truth;
2. hypotheses, quantifiers, notation, and object introductions; for an existing or collaborative manuscript, compare every symbol in the added text with the authoritative source and remove unauthorized aliases or one-use names;
3. proof dependencies and any omitted difficult step;
4. proof-thread continuity: the target remains visible, persistent objects keep stable names, technical detours return to the formula they justify, and the closing step combines earlier outputs on the same object;
5. proof factorization: long technical passages with a genuine interface are lemmas or appendices, dependencies are stated before use, and short calculations are not over-fragmented;
6. citations against the claims they support;
7. abstract, introduction, and conclusion against the body;
8. language-only edits for semantic drift and rhetorical weakening: established contributions remain direct, necessary scope remains attached, and generic or repeated disclaimers have not been added merely because internal checking was cautious;
9. visual claims, captions, accessibility, and reproducibility when figures or tables changed;
10. cover letters and referee responses against the manuscript, decision letter, and current venue requirements;
11. changed LaTeX and all affected versions with the repository's build procedure.

For a first draft, check the completed artifact against its source mathematics and requested scope, then assess the [exposition dimensions](exposition-and-structure.md#what-a-writing-improvement-must-accomplish): does it develop the problem and argument, explain decisive choices, and provide the promised statements and proofs? An outline or accurate inventory of results does not satisfy a drafting request. For a revision, also compare the original and revised text, identify the reading difficulty resolved and any cost in length, notation or navigation, and revert gratuitous changes. These are editorial judgments unless actual reader evidence was collected.

For file edits, report changed paths, checks run, unresolved mathematical or bibliographic risks, and the preserved evidence status. A successful build certifies typesetting, not mathematics.
