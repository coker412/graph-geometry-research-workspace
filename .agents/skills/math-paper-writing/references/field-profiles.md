Preserve the target manuscript's mathematics, notation, accepted abstract and venue conventions. Do not load all profiles for an ordinary edit.

Each profile now has **twenty distinct complete training works and all five Writing DNA synthesis documents**. Four additional works, one per author, remain outside those eighty training works. The full works were obtained and annotated through selected close reading of openings, transitions, structure and endings; this is not exhaustive proof verification. Five training works per author received systematic whole-document visual inspection. Scripted prose diagnostics are reported with extraction limits, not converted into language targets.

The [Writing DNA mathematical adaptation](style-distillation.md) supplies the six layers and application modes. Each lightweight profile entry links `language-dna.md`, `structure-patterns.md`, `cognitive-framework.md`, `visual-style-guide.md` and `Writing-DNA.md`. The structure report contains all twenty sources, complete author lists, dates, architectures and passage locations. For targeted borrowing, read the relevant decision and source passages. For full-profile drafting, read all five reports and five relevant full sources. These packs provide links and original analysis, not bundled source papers.

The [application and check record](profile-applications.md) comes from the earlier three-paper pilot. Its reserved papers have since been read; that record is not a fresh blind test of the expanded models. Neither twenty works nor a writing award establishes author-wide fidelity, independence of observations or author endorsement. Collaborations and related series are identified; their prose is not attributed sentence by sentence to the named author.


## Why these authors

All four received the **Leroy P. Steele Prize for Mathematical Exposition**: Milnor (2004), Stanley (2001), Mumford (2007), Evans (2023). This is a reason to inspect their writing, not evidence for any particular rule in these packs. The awards often concern books or a wider expository contribution, rather than the research papers selected here. See [Gowers's account hosted by the Abel Prize](https://abelprisen.no/sites/default/files/2021-04/The%20Work%20of%20John%20Milnor%202011.pdf), [Stanley's MIT profile](https://math.mit.edu/directory/profile.html?pid=263), [Mumford's Brown profile](https://appliedmath.brown.edu/people/david-mumford), and [Berkeley's Evans announcement](https://math.berkeley.edu/node/1527).

## What stays in the core

The core already covers meaningful examples, proof interfaces, precise scope and returning from reductions. These packs add concrete ways to apply those principles. They do not add mandatory introductions, sentence lengths, example counts, visual styles or limitation paragraphs. They belong to research-paper writing; beginner study editions use their separate learning workflow.

To distill a different author or one's own drafts, invoke the existing distillation mode, identify the corpus and intended use, and save a separate optional profile. The standard study uses twenty or more works. An explicitly chosen small-sample mode also supports provisional profiles from three or more; one or two works support local analysis only. Do not substitute these smaller modes for a requested standard study. A personal profile is not automatically part of the distributable skill.
