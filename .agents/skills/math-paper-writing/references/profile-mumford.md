# Writing DNA: Mumford

**Classical algebraic geometry, 1961–1982. Standard twenty-work corpus, with five synthesis documents.** Use this optional profile when the task is to explain why a formulation or change of category makes the original question tractable.

The corpus contains 12 sole-authored and 8 collaborative works; the additional prior pilot comparison is not counted toward twenty. Genre counts: 20 research paper. Complete files, selected close reading and per-work structure annotations support the profile; this is not exhaustive proof verification or independent author-fidelity validation.
